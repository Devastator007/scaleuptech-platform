Essential storage

Our products may use cookies or local browser storage that are strictly necessary for sign-in, security, language preference, session continuity, and core functionality. Disabling them may prevent a product from working correctly.

Analytics and optional technologies

If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking, we will identify it and request consent where required. We do not use optional tracking merely because you visit this marketing website.

Managing preferences

You can clear or block browser storage through your browser settings. Product-specific controls, when available, take precedence for optional technologies. Essential security records may still be retained outside your browser.
