Privacy Policy

How ScaleUp Tech collects, uses, protects, and retains personal information.

Last updated: 29 July 2026
Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceCookie NoticeSecurity

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, account details, support messages, and product inputs. We may also collect limited technical data needed to secure and operate our services, including device, browser, network, and usage information.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve our products, authenticate users, process support requests, protect accounts, comply with legal obligations, and communicate service-related updates. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and processors

We share information only with service providers needed to operate a product, when you direct us to, or when required by law. Providers are expected to process information only for the contracted purpose and with appropriate safeguards.

Retention and your choices

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the service, security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. Some records may be retained where the law or legitimate security needs require it.
